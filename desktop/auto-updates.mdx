The desktop app auto-updates in the background across three release channels:

| Channel | Cadence | Who it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `prod` | Stable releases only. | Everyone by default. |
| `beta` | Release candidates. | Teams that want a heads-up on the next stable. |
| `dev` | Nightly-ish. | Early adopters and contributors. |

## Change channels

Switch channels from the app's settings pane. On next launch, the app checks the selected channel for a newer build; you can also trigger a check manually.

## Under the hood

Updates are downloaded in the background and applied on next launch. If an update fails to apply, the app keeps the previous version and shows an error — there's no in-between state.
